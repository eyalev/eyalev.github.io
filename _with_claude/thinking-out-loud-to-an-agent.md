I do most of my work now by talking to an AI coding agent in voice notes. **I can start with a half-formed idea, change my mind in the middle of a sentence, and the agent still finds what I'm asking for and does it.** Typing the same request would take longer, and I'd usually cut the idea short before it was clear even to me.

## Speed

Most people speak about three times faster than they type, and on a phone the difference is bigger. A two-minute voice note holds more than I'd ever write in a message.

## The mess is fine

A voice note has repeated words, a lot of "maybe" and ideas that change direction halfway. A good agent reads past that, asks when something is ambiguous, and turns it into code, a plan or a draft. The posts in this section started that way.

## Explaining helps

Explaining something, even to an agent, makes you notice what you actually want. I often get to the real request at the end of a voice note, after a minute of circling around it.

## Where typing wins

Short precise instructions, a name, a number or a path, are faster to type than to say, and a finished text still needs someone to read the draft carefully and cut what isn't right.
