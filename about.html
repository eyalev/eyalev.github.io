about

I'm Eyal. I write software, mostly backend and mostly tools. I'm Israeli, and since 2024 I live in Portugal, near Lisbon, with my family.

I started as a Linux sysadmin at the Technion, which is probably why I still reach for the terminal first and end up building things that live there. From there I did web work in PHP and Rails, and then spent six and a half years at BreezoMeter doing Python backends for air quality and pollen APIs, from small startup through to the Google acquisition. After that, Changebase, then Check Point, where I worked on data pipelines for security products.

Since the beginning of 2024 I've been working on my own things. Most of them collect information that is scattered across a lot of places and keep it attributed, so you can check where anything came from. Some are language learning (Lingush, How to Learn), and a good part is tooling for AI coding agents, which started because I was running several at once and could not tell what any of them were doing without walking back to my desk. They are all on the front page.
